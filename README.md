I study Data Science and Artificial Intelligence at Hanoi University of Science and Technology. At the Computational Intelligence Optimization Lab, I work on routing and scheduling with mathematical programming and heuristic search. I’m also interested in how models make decisions with incomplete information.

- **🔍 [DGSP](https://github.com/kongwoang/DGSP)** — Where should a driving system look more closely? DGSP places hypothetical objects in a scene and checks whether they would change the planner’s action before spending computation on a closer look.
- **🛡️ [GUARD](https://github.com/kongwoang/GUARD)** — When a multimodal model loses an input, when is it worth correcting its prediction? GUARD uses conformal risk control to decide when to apply a correction while keeping the model weights fixed.
